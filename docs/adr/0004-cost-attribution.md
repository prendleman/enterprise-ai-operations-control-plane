# ADR 0004: Cost Attribution

- **Status:** Accepted

## Context
AI spend is often invisible until invoices surprise Finance.

## Decision
Simulate usage allocation by business unit, use case, provider, model, and month, with 80/100/120% budget alerts. Document real FinOps integration points.

## Alternatives Considered
Ignore cost until cloud bills arrive; require live billing APIs for demos.

## Consequences
Executive cost views work offline; not a claim of real billing accuracy.
