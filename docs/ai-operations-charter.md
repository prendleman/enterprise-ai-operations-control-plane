# AI Operations Charter

## Mission

Provide a governed operating layer so AI initiatives are intake-managed, risk-reviewed, cost-visible, and measurable — instead of remaining disconnected experiments.

## Scope

**Owns:** intake facilitation, model/provider standards, sandbox eligibility policy, agent registration standards, risk/exception workflow design, cost visibility patterns, adoption and portfolio reporting.

**Does not own:** every model training pipeline, every business product roadmap, production cloud account ownership, or replacing executive judgment.

## Services

AI intake, architecture review, provider/model approval, sandbox provisioning guidance, agent registration, risk review, exception management, AI cost governance, usage analytics, production-readiness review, adoption measurement, AI standards.

## Decision rights

See `docs/decision-rights.md`.

## Stakeholders

AI Operations, Security, Privacy, Enterprise Architecture, Engineering, Business Owners, Finance/FinOps, Legal, Platform.

## Operating cadence

- Weekly intake triage
- Biweekly risk/exception review
- Monthly portfolio and cost review
- Quarterly production eligibility and retirement review

## KPIs

Approved use cases, pilot-to-production conversion, budget variance, open exceptions aging, high-risk inventory, active agents with human-review coverage, adoption by business unit.

## Escalation

CRITICAL or blocked initiatives escalate to AI Operations + Security (+ Architecture when autonomy/write access is involved).
